About Our School

Our History

New Horizon Public School, Darbhanga was established in 2000 with the vision of delivering quality education rooted in strong values, discipline, and academic excellence. Over the years, the school has grown into a respected institution known for its holistic approach to learning.

We believe education is not just about academics, but about shaping responsible, confident, and compassionate individuals prepared to face global challenges.
